# Shufti Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) Policy

## 1. Introduction
At Shufti, the safety and well-being of our users—especially minors—are of paramount importance. We are committed to creating a secure platform that strictly prohibits any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation. This policy outlines our efforts to prevent, detect, and respond to any content or behavior that could facilitate such abuse.

## 2. Policy Statement
Shufti maintains a zero-tolerance approach toward all forms of child sexual abuse and exploitation. We are dedicated to complying with all applicable international, national, and local laws in combating child sexual abuse. Any instances of suspected or actual abuse will be promptly and rigorously investigated. If necessary, we will cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and relevant authorities.

## 3. Definitions
- **Child Sexual Abuse:** Any sexual activity involving a minor, including any representation of such activity.
- **Exploitation:** Taking advantage of a child for sexual purposes, including the production, distribution, or possession of abusive material.
- **User-Generated Content (UGC):** Any content submitted by users through our platform, including text, images, videos, or any other form of digital media.

## 4. Reporting Mechanisms
- **In-App Reporting:**  
  Shufti provides an easy-to-access in-app mechanism that allows users and community members to report any content or behavior suspected of involving child sexual abuse or exploitation and to block users if necessary. All reports and blocking actions are treated with the utmost seriousness and confidentiality.
- **Direct Contact:**  
  Users can also directly contact our dedicated abuse response team via [abuse@shuftiapp.com](mailto:abuse@shuftiapp.com) to report concerns. We encourage anyone who has information related to CSAE to report it immediately.

## 5. Response and Investigation
- **Immediate Action:**  
  Upon receipt of any report related to CSAE, Shufti will conduct a swift preliminary review. If the content or behavior is confirmed to pose a risk or directly involve child abuse, immediate steps will be taken to remove or disable access to the offending material.
- **Collaboration with Authorities:**  
  We are committed to cooperating fully with law enforcement and other competent authorities. Shufti will provide all necessary assistance and documentation as required under the law to aid in the investigation and prosecution of offenders.

## 6. User Responsibilities
- **Compliance:**  
  By using Shufti, users agree to abide by our CSAE Policy and our broader Terms of Service. Users are expected to refrain from posting or sharing any content that can be reasonably construed as abusive, exploitative, or otherwise harmful to minors.
- **Reporting Suspicious Activity:**  
  All members of our community have a vital role in ensuring a safe environment. If you suspect or witness any behavior or content that may lead to the exploitation or abuse of children, please report it immediately through our designated channels.

## 7. Policy Enforcement
Any user found to be in violation of this policy may be subject to immediate removal of content, suspension, or termination of access to Shufti. Repeat or severe violations may also be reported to the appropriate legal authorities for further action.

## 8. Amendments and Updates
This policy is subject to review and modification at regular intervals or in response to new legal or regulatory requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating this policy on our website.

## 9. Contact Information
For any questions, concerns, or further information regarding our CSAE Policy, please contact our Abuse Response Team at: [abuse@shuftiapp.com](mailto:abuse@shuftiapp.com)

**Disclaimer:**  
The information provided in this policy is intended for general informational purposes only and does not constitute legal advice. Shufti strongly recommends that you seek qualified legal counsel to ensure that your policies and practices comply with all relevant laws and regulations.

This CSAE Policy reflects Shufti's unwavering commitment to protecting children from abuse and exploitation. We appreciate the continued trust and cooperation of our users as we strive to maintain a safe and secure online environment.

# Commitment to Taking Appropriate Action to Address Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)

When Shufti becomes aware of the presence of CSAM in our app, we will promptly and decisively act to remove the offending content using our in-app mechanism. Our approach aligns with our published standards, procedures, and all relevant laws. Upon receiving verified reports of CSAM through our in-app feedback or reporting mechanism, we commit to:

- **Immediate Removal:** Utilizing our in-app mechanism to swiftly delete or disable access to any reported CSAM content.
- **Compliance with Legal Obligations:** Working in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, which may include notifying the relevant law enforcement authorities.
- **Consistent Application of Policy:** Following the procedures outlined in our policies to ensure a secure environment for our users.

While we do not mandate a specific methodology, our in-app mechanism ensures that we can promptly act in accordance with our stated policies and legal requirements whenever CSAM is reported within our app.
